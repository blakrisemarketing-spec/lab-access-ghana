Who we are

Lab Access is a licensed diagnostic centre in Accra, built around one idea: getting accurate results to patients and doctors quickly, without the bureaucracy.

We run a full in-house laboratory — haematology, biochemistry, microbiology, immunology, and molecular diagnostics. Most routine panels return the same day. For patients who cannot visit, our nursing team collects samples at home.

Corporate clients use us for pre-employment screening, annual wellness checks, and occupational health programmes. We structure reports for HR and insurance compliance.

Common questions

Do I need to fast before my test?

It depends on the test. Fasting blood sugar and lipid profiles require 8–12 hours of fasting. Most other tests do not. We will confirm when you book.

How soon will I receive my results?

Most routine tests return the same day. Complex panels and cultures may take 24–48 hours. We send results via WhatsApp, email, or printed copy — your choice.

Do you offer home sample collection?

Yes. Our nursing team collects samples at your home or office in Accra and Tema. Book at least 24 hours ahead.

What should I bring?

Bring a valid ID. If your test is employer-sponsored, bring your referral form or company letter. Insurance cards accepted where applicable.

Do you accept health insurance?

We work with most major health insurers in Ghana. Call ahead with your policy number and we will confirm coverage before your visit.

Can you handle corporate health screening?

Yes. We run on-site and off-site screening programmes for companies of all sizes. We structure reports for HR compliance and provide bulk pricing.
